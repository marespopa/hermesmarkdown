# use-edge-swipe

Description: `useEdgeSwipe` opens the left drawer on a right swipe of 60px or more that starts within 20px of the left edge, and the outline drawer on a left swipe from the right edge. A mostly vertical move never counts, and it is ignored while a field or contenteditable has focus (keyboard up) or text is selected. `useSwipeToClose(toward, onClose)` returns touch handlers for an open panel. `classifySwipe` and `edgeOf` are the pure thresholds.
